WHAT THE WORDS MEAN

Four ways solar can cost less.

Not every program is available everywhere. Use these definitions to know what to look for.

1

Upfront rebate

Money taken off the purchase price or paid back after installation.

2

Tax benefit

A state credit or an exemption that may reduce sales or property tax.

3

Bill credits

Net metering may credit electricity your panels send to the grid.

4

Income-qualified help

Some programs provide extra support based on income or neighborhood eligibility.
