How your personal results work

Your daily records are matched by date to historical temperature from Open-Meteo. WattWeather calculates descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, 65°F heating/cooling degree scores, and IQR anomaly flags in your browser.

Why IQR? It flags unusually high records without assuming energy use follows a perfect bell curve and is less distorted by extreme values than a z-score.

Why linear regression? It keeps the temperature relationship visible and explainable. A more flexible model could fit noise and would be harder to justify with limited household data.

Weather is only one possible driver. Household behavior, rate plans, appliances, building size, and billing-period length can also change usage.
