What does the connection score mean?

This is a Pearson correlation between annual city temperature and statewide average household electricity use. A score near 1 or -1 means they changed together more often; a score near 0 means the relationship was weak. Correlation does not prove that weather caused the change.

How these public results work

Open-Meteo supplies city search, current weather, forecasts, solar conditions, and historical temperature. A published U.S. EIA snapshot supplies statewide residential prices and usage.

The comparison pairs annual city temperature with statewide average household electricity use. It is historical context—not a personal bill analysis or proof of cause.
